SQUM Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026 · Gnarly Shaped Inc.

SQUM (the "game") is a number puzzle game made by Gnarly Shaped Inc. ("we"). This policy explains what information the game handles, why, and what you can do about it.

In short: you can play without registering. We keep your saved game on our servers so it isn't lost, we keep a record of purchases, and the one optional ad in the game is served by Google. We don't sell your information.

1. Information we collect

Your saved game

The first time the game goes online, it makes a guest account for you: a random ID with no name or email attached. Under that ID, our servers store:

  • Your progress: levels unlocked, stars, solve times, crowns, badges, your daily bonus calendar, and which tips and prompts you have seen
  • Your coins and items, and a history of how they were earned and spent (with the time of each change)
  • Your game settings and the language the game is shown in
  • When the account was made

The same information is also kept on your device, so you can play offline.

If you sign in with Apple or Google

Signing in is optional. If you do, Apple or Google tells us:

  • Your email address (with Apple, this can be a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email")
  • The name on your account, if the provider shares it
  • An ID that tells us it is the same Apple or Google account next time

We use these only to bring your saved game back when you sign in on any device. We never see your Apple or Google password.

Purchases

Purchases are paid through the App Store or Google Play. We never receive your card number or billing details. For each purchase we store the store's transaction ID, what was bought, which store it came from and when, so we can add what you bought to your save and restore it if something goes wrong.

Ads

The game has one ad, and it is optional: you can choose to watch it to continue a level after running out of hearts. It is served by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address, general device information, and how you interact with the ad. On iOS, the advertising ID is shared only if you allow tracking when asked. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. If you never watch the ad, no ad is shown to you.

Technical information

Like any online service, our servers receive your IP address and basic details of each request (such as the time, and the app and system version) when the game syncs. We use this to run the service, keep it secure and fix problems.

Emails you send us

Tapping Feedback in the game opens your mail app with the game version and your device's system version filled in. We use your email only to reply to you, and we delete it on request once we're done.

2. What we don't collect

  • The game does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We do not ask for your phone number, address or date of birth.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

3. How we use information

  • To save your game and bring it back on any device
  • To deliver what you buy, and to restore purchases
  • To keep the game fair, for example by checking coin and item changes on the server
  • To show the optional ad
  • To keep the service running and secure, and to fix problems
  • To answer you when you write to us

Where the law asks for a legal basis, we rely on our agreement with you (providing the game and your purchases), our legitimate interest in running a fair and secure game, and your consent (for personalised ads).

4. Who we share it with

We share information only with the companies that help us run the game, and only as much as each one needs:

  • Supabase: hosts our database and sign-in. It stores your account and saved game.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in, if you choose it, and payments through the App Store and Google Play.
  • RevenueCat: confirms purchases with the App Store or Google Play. It receives your account ID and purchase details.
  • Google AdMob: serves the optional ad, as described above.

Each of these handles information under its own privacy policy. We may also share information if the law requires it, or if the game is transferred to another company, in which case this policy continues to apply to your information.

These companies may process information in countries other than the one you live in, including the United States and Canada.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your account and saved game are kept until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, your login, saved game, coins and items, their history and your purchase records are deleted from our servers.
  • Server logs are kept for a short time and then deleted.
  • We may keep information longer where the law requires it, such as tax records of sales.

On iPhone and iPad, your guest login is kept in the device's Keychain, so your save can return if you delete and reinstall the game. On Android, it is removed when you uninstall the game.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: if you are signed in, go to Settings → Save progress → Delete account. This deletes your account and everything stored with it from our servers.
  • Reset your game: Settings → Reset all progress puts your save back to a fresh start.
  • Sign out: Settings → Save progress → Sign out. Your save stays on our servers for the next time you sign in.
  • Ads: you can change tracking and ad personalisation in your device's settings, and you can simply not watch the ad.
  • Guests: if you play as a guest and want your data deleted, email us from the game's Feedback row and we will help.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or limit how we use it, or to withdraw consent. To use any of these rights, email us at the address below. You may also complain to your local privacy authority.

7. Children's privacy

The game is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the age your country sets instead). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections, and each player can read only their own saved game. Your login is stored in your device's secure storage. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and keep as little of it as we can.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
